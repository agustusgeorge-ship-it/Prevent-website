Know what to look for

Types of danger

One sign on its own does not mean someone is at risk. A pattern of changes may be a reason to check in and seek advice.

01

Suspicious links

Unexpected messages, links or requests for personal information can be used to scam, manipulate or radicalise.

02

Friends or family having sudden changes

Noticeable changes in behaviour, language, appearance or who someone spends time with may need a gentle conversation.

03

Too good to be true

Promises of easy money, belonging or instant answers can hide pressure, exploitation or harmful influence.

04

Radicalisation

Being drawn towards increasingly extreme ideas or groups can happen online or in person. Support is available early.

05

Pressure to keep secrets

Someone asking for secrecy, isolating a person from trusted support, or discouraging questions can be a warning sign.

06

Us and them language

Increasingly hostile language about a group, combined with claims that violence or harm is justified, deserves attention.
